## English project description (edit only after honest live validation)

FoodRecall AI is an evidence-grounded investigation workbench for food recall response. It combines read-only source investigation, explainable lot-level review, temporal evidence change detection, human approval and independent readback in a safety-oriented workflow. An investigation records the tools it selected, sources actually retrieved, known contradictions and unresolved uncertainty. A selective Decision Validity Engine invalidates prior approvals when material supporting evidence changes, while preserving irrelevant-note changes. The local QA workflow supports an auditable proposal–approval–synthetic containment–verification cycle. FDA public enforcement records are separately archived with provenance, and potential Zetaris cross-source lot links remain review candidates until verified against authorized operational identifiers. A configurable NVIDIA Nemotron tool-calling route is included, but cannot be claimed live until the model's real tool selection has been observed. The project is currently a local training prototype and never executes real warehouse actions.
